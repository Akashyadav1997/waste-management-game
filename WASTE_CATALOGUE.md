# Waste curriculum

The game contains 76 distinct items. The kitchen opens with the original six props and uses six-item rounds; other chapters introduce three items at a time; all items must be sorted before the chapter completes. Existing chapter IDs and saved unlocks are retained. Replaying a completed chapter starts its expanded curriculum from round one.

The supplied project colours remain green (wet), blue (dry), yellow (sanitary), and red (hazardous household). As clarified by the user, insecticide sprays, expired medicines, tube lights and CFLs use red; personal-cleaning tissues and cotton swabs / ear buds use yellow. Rinsing and wrapping are preparation instructions, not extra bins. Clean paper / unused tissues remain blue; used cleaning tissues and used sanitary pads remain yellow. A paper wrapper does not change the category of its contents.

Reference: [CPCB solid-waste portal](https://swm.cpcb.gov.in/) and [government four-stream guidance](https://ipr.nagaland.gov.in/index.php/HOME-DEPARTMENT-INFORMS-CITIZENS-ON-SOLID-WASTE-MANAGEMENT). Bin colours and the household bandage / razor examples follow the project curriculum, not a universal collection policy. Item-specific instructions distinguish blood-soaked dressings, chemical-soaked wipes, sharp objects, and designated e-waste collection.

Natural utensils are shown as uncoated leaf or fibre products; the game does not classify plastic-lined cups or industrially compostable plastic as ordinary wet waste. Pencil waste is shown as untreated wooden shavings. “Aluminium foil/Tap” is interpreted as foil and a can pull tab. Reusable clothes should be donated before disposal.
